About Me

I am an applied AI researcher and data scientist specializing in computer vision, 3D reconstruction, and large language models. Previously, I interned at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, where I designed systems for reconstructing 3D satellite models from limited imagery using 3D Gaussian Splatting and achieved a 95% mean IoU with fine-tuned SAM 2 segmentation. I hold an M.S. in Applied Data Science from the University of Southern California (Magna Cum Laude, GPA 3.90, Phi Beta Kappa).
